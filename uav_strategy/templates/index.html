Under the hood

Evolution, not enumeration.

01

Hybrid chromosome

Deployment coordinates, ordered waypoints, per-node sensor power, per-leg speed and fleet activation are encoded in one 190-gene strand.

02

Five objectives

Coverage and detection are maximised; energy, flight time and hazard exposure are minimised under range, no-fly and coverage constraints.

03

Spatial operators

Geometric BLX-α, whole-segment swap and route-splice crossover combine with adaptive mutation and elite preservation.

04

Pareto archive

Every run returns the selected compromise plus the non-dominated alternatives that trade coverage, energy and safety.
